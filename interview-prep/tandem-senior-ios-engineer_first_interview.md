# Tandem - First Recruiter Interview Preparation

## Tell Me About Yourself

I am a Senior iOS Engineer with more than 20 years of software development experience and around 15 years focused on iOS development.

During the last years I have been working on SAP Concur, one of the largest business travel and expense management platforms. My work included architecture design, feature development, accessibility improvements, testing, CI/CD pipelines and code quality initiatives.

I enjoy working on long-term products, modernizing existing codebases and helping teams deliver high-quality software.

In addition to my daily engineering work, I actively explore how AI can improve mobile user experiences. I am particularly interested in how AI can make iOS and Android interfaces more adaptive, reduce UI complexity and help users achieve their goals faster without navigating through complex menus and workflows.

At the moment I am looking for a product company where I can have a strong technical impact while continuing to grow as a senior engineer.

---

## Why Are You Looking For A New Opportunity?

I have learned a lot from SAP Concur and worked on a large enterprise product with millions of users and complex business requirements.

Now I would like to work closer to end users and have a stronger impact on the overall product experience.

Tandem combines mobile development, communication and language learning, which makes it particularly interesting to me.

---

## Why Tandem?

One reason is very personal.

I moved to Germany a few years ago and I am actively learning German myself. Because of that I understand the challenges language learners face.

I also like that Tandem is a mobile-first product used by millions of people around the world.

The opportunity to contribute to a product that directly helps people learn languages and connect with others is very appealing to me.

## What Interests You Most About This Role?

The job description mentions modernizing the iOS codebase, improving architecture and driving engineering excellence.

These are exactly the areas where I have the strongest experience.

I enjoy improving maintainability, scalability and code quality while still delivering business value and new features.
